# Memo from the Director

**To:** Project team-05
**From:** Director, Greenfield Tool Library
**Re:** Rules for our new system

Thank you for taking on our database. A few things specific to our branch:

1. **Borrowing limit.** A member may have at most **4** tools checked out at the same time.
   Our volunteers have enforced this by hand, and we want the new system to enforce it automatically.

2. **Name changes.** One of our long-time members, Carlos Novak (`carlos.novak781@proton.me`), recently asked us to
   update their name. In the old loan log we would have had to fix every one of their rows by hand.
   Please make sure that never happens again.

3. **Late fees.** Our volunteers typed late fees into the loan log themselves, and I suspect many are wrong.
   The rule has always been: each day a tool comes back after its due date costs that category's daily
   late fee. Nothing is charged for tools returned on time.

4. **Certifications.** Some tool categories are dangerous enough that members must complete a safety
   class before borrowing from them. The categories file says which.

The spreadsheets were exported on June 30, 2026. Apologies in advance for the state they are in.
